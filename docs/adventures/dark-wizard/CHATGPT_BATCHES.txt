Make 5 separate landscape 3:2 picture-book paintings for a 7-year-old, one after another, in the warm painted style of the sheet. Keep Artus, Pip (big orange dragon), Jet (small red dragon), Grandma (old pink dragon), Oren and Gus exactly as on the sheet. Grax: tall thin dark wizard, long black coat, green cat eyes. Rook: thin nervous young man, old grey coat. No text in the pictures. Give each picture the file name shown.

dw-storm-a — Night in Grandma's cosy cottage by the fire. Little Jet flies fast near the wooden roof beams; soup has splashed on the floor; Grandma laughs; Artus and big Pip laugh too. Window with a big moon.

dw-storm-b — Midnight. Grax stands in the open bedroom window in his black coat, green eyes shining, one hand raised. Black smoke forms a ball around little Jet, who beats his wings inside it. Pip breathes a flame at it; Artus in nightclothes and his red scarf points a light spell.

dw-trail-a — Oren's tall stone tower at sunset. Inside, old Oren shows a big open book to Artus; Pip listens sadly; Gus the giant looks in through a big window.

dw-trail-b — Top of Oren's tower: Oren and Artus look into a big bowl of magic water showing a black island with a dark castle; Artus holds a small silver torch.

dw-jungle-a — A wild green forest of huge trees and vines. A huge orange tiger with a green cape on its back steps onto the path; Artus rides Pip, both stop in surprise. A line of black smoke in the sky above the trees.
