# Core 200 and September 22 play feedback

The playable curriculum now contains all 200 unique words from the supplied `BLITZWORD_CURRICULUM_200_1000(1)(1).xlsx`, sheet `BlitzWord 200`. This is the approved mixed curriculum, not a frequency ranking. Existing Chapter 1 targets, order, observations and identifiers remain intact. The other 170 targets are appended in workbook order, each with a fixed sentence and three distinct distractors. Close real words are preferred; the remaining spelling contrasts are distractors only.

Seven chapters contain 30, 30, 30, 30, 30, 30 and 20 words. Each has five places. A place requires all its words introduced, at least 80% practiced successfully twice, and its secured checkpoint. A final encounter completes each chapter. The map opens after the finale. No date or elapsed-time gate blocks story chapters. The web preview makes all 200 words playable as requested; it does not grant a purchase entitlement or unlock Ride/Fly.

The five-hour test runs 1,800 responses with deliberate mistakes, help, an unattended interval, a device-sleep interval, and a saved reload. It verifies exactly 300 interaction-confirmed practice minutes, all 200 words encountered, all 35 places cleared, all seven finales completed, continued review, permanent XP and at most six new words per session. This is simulated capacity and progression coverage, not a measured claim that an individual child needs five hours to complete the story. About 45 seven-minute sessions is a practical five-hour plan. Faster children can finish the story sooner and retain continued practice and number duels.

## Combat and saves

An encounter resolves only when enemy or hero health is zero. The seven-minute target is checked at combat completion; results still show the battle reward. The former seven-attempt demo cutoff is also removed. Help remains free and can extend a demonstration. Voluntary pauses preserve the exact living encounter, and a saved-practice screen is not a victory or defeat.

Each opponent type has a fixed three-point health interval, starting with 3–5, then 6–8, 9–11 and so on. Named larger variants preserve the creature family and get their own identity and interval. Opponent choices exclude recent appearance families; stronger choices therefore show a different creature. A pending fight from an older save retains its exact health and identity until it ends. The five existing creature artworks are reused for variants; this release does not claim 100 unique enemy illustrations.

Legacy `chapterComplete` maps to completed Chapter 1. Existing profiles, XP, growth stages, checkpoints, observations and active questions are retained. Assessment pools and scoring are unchanged. The original saved question keeps its exposure when the child chooses a new speed.

## Child screens

Long-press selection and the copy/lookup callout are disabled in play, while text entry and parent information retain normal selection. The map uses a short chapter name, one Play button, and a larger hero marker with a solid backing so it remains visible over rivers. Controls share a single toolbar. Speed choices use short labels and small pace marks; selecting an available speed closes the panel. Ride/Fly retain their existing locks.

The neutral mask is one fixed, static warm spiral. It never reveals target length. Reading and choices still suppress combat motion and ambience. Andika is bundled locally under its SIL Open Font License, with ligatures disabled and explicit character spacing for targets and alternatives.

Pip opens a growth panel. XP, active minutes and elapsed days have separate labelled meters. The compact bar and result bar show XP progress, not the minimum of unrelated prerequisites. The existing 250/750/1500 XP and practice-minute requirements, fourteen-day minimum and rideable chapter requirement remain unchanged. Results show the total earned battle XP, including the associated completed multiplication round, plus progress towards the next stage.

## Artwork and review

`assets/chapter-scenes.webp` contains six new equal scenery tiles. The first chapter retains the original forest. Subsequent chapters use river, oak, ruins, moonlit wood, crystal cave and hilltop keep scenes. `assets/teaching/core-teaching.webp` contains twelve teaching scenes: cottage/cat/ball/bell; family; car; ship/shark; queen/sword; wizard/unicorn; snake/rain; ghost/bat; lion; farm animals; pan; child drawing a map. They are used only in teaching, never beside answer choices.

Both atlases were generated with the built-in image tool and inspected as full contact sheets. Output images retain their original proportions; WebP encoding reduces transfer size. Scene coordinates use a virtual 1536×2048 view box for the teaching sheet and 1536×1024 for scenery. Familiar Pip pictures remain unchanged. Sentences with abstract words provide the meaning context; the supporting image alone is not treated as evidence of understanding. No external pedagogical validation is claimed.

The atlas prompts specified consistent hand-painted storybook scenery, no text/UI, and exact equal cells. Scenery excludes characters and leaves open ground for combat sprites. Teaching uses ordinary story characters rather than regenerating the selected hero or Pip.

## Verification boundaries

Automated checks cover content uniqueness and allocation, health intervals, living-combat guards, old-save migration, seven-chapter progression, reward idempotence, teaching/correction return, speed selection, countdown scoring and parent time accounting. All 72 tests and 16 DOM flow groups passed in the gameplay release check. An independent read of the supplied workbook confirmed an exact 200-word set match, with no missing or extra targets. The five-hour run covered 1,800 responses, 200 words, 35 places, seven chapter finales and 300 confirmed minutes, with 25 additional wall-clock minutes excluded as idle/sleep.

The recovered narration expansion is deferred at the user's request. This gameplay release retains the 165 existing clips and their verified hashes. The other words and sentences use the established browser-speech fallback without requesting missing recordings. Full recorded coverage and synchronized word-boundary highlighting remain outstanding. Physical iPad typography, long-press behavior and listening suitability still require device observation.

The expanded audio packs are excluded from this release. Pip is the provisional child-facing title, not an approved commercial rename; buttons and short labels use the same parchment/forest palette. Chapter titles continue to identify the current place.
