    How time is counted

    Reading and teaching time is confirmed by the next answer, Ready, replay, continue, Home or pause action. After 30 seconds without a game action, the game pauses and discards the unconfirmed interval. Menus, results, background tabs and device sleep do not count. Idle excluded shows discarded reading intervals; paused and background duration is not measured. Assessment and demo time are shown separately and do not advance Pip’s growth.

    This measures interaction-confirmed time, not attention. A reading interval longer than 30 seconds is conservatively excluded. Closing or reloading can lose the last unconfirmed interval.

    Earlier time records could include waiting. They are kept separately and do not count towards growth: .

    Multiplication time

    The bonus uses 60 unpaused, foreground seconds. Pause, Home, hidden tabs and device sleep stop the countdown; returning does not refill it. Correct answers earn 1 XP. Keypad actions confirm active multiplication time separately from reading, and both count towards growth. Unconfirmed time at the deadline is excluded from active play.
