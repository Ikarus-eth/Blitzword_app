    How time is counted

    Reading and teaching time is confirmed by the next answer, Ready, replay, continue, Home or pause action. After 30 seconds without a game action, the game pauses and discards the unconfirmed interval. Menus, results, background tabs and device sleep do not count. Idle excluded shows discarded reading intervals; paused and background duration is not measured. Assessment and demo time are shown separately and do not advance Pip’s growth.

    This measures interaction-confirmed time, not attention. A reading interval longer than 30 seconds is conservatively excluded. Closing or reloading can lose the last unconfirmed interval.

    Earlier time records could include waiting. They are kept separately and do not count towards growth: .

    Word selection

    All 200 Core words remain playable across seven campaigns, each with five chapters. A battle is one enemy; a chapter is one map field. A new chapter needs at least ten interaction-confirmed active minutes, three reading wins, a completed number duel and its learning objectives. A duel loss does not erase reading progress. If the duel finishes before ten minutes, another battle continues the chapter. Cleared chapters stay cleared. After the story, ten-minute review chapters continue the daily goal. Aim for one chapter a day; there is no daily limit. Core 200 is a curriculum selection, not the 200 most frequent English words: it combines high-frequency words, phonics examples and forest vocabulary. In the supplied workbook, water has Fry rank 84; bird is selected for phonics and the story theme. Assessment and saved learning records are unchanged.

    Multiplication time

    The bonus uses 60 unpaused, foreground seconds. Pause, Home, hidden tabs and device sleep stop the countdown; returning does not refill it. New rounds score +1 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong answer, including below zero. Earned XP is permanent; correct answers earn 1 XP before the daily multiplier. Older personal records and any round already in progress retain their original scoring. Answer selections confirm active multiplication time separately from reading, and both count towards the daily bonus; growth itself depends only on XP. Unconfirmed time at the deadline is excluded from active play.

    XP and daily bonus

    Reading: 3 XP per unaided correct answer. A word earns 8 extra XP once after three separated successes across two battles; its first successful review at least 24 hours later earns 4. Reliable Run-or-faster review earns 1 extra XP when at least 18 of the last 20 independent answers at that speed were correct. Chapter accuracy earns 10 XP at 90% or 5 at 80%, with at least ten independent answers. At ten active minutes, 20 daily XP is awarded once and correct-answer XP is multiplied by 1.75 for the rest of that local calendar day. Milestone bonuses are not multiplied. Fractions are retained; the child display shows whole XP. Menus, idle time, demo and assessment do not earn the daily reward.

    Growth is XP-only. The 3,000 / 8,900 / 13,400 thresholds are calibrated against a deterministic practice model, not a guarantee: real progress depends on accuracy, speed and retention. Earned forms and old XP remain intact.
