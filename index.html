    How Pip grows

    Each unaided correct battle answer and correct multiplication answer earns 1 XP, including review. Reading speed earns no extra XP. Help is free. Earned XP and growth are never lost.

    Young Pip needs 250 XP, 250 active practice minutes and at least 14 days since practice began. Reading and multiplication practice both count. Older forms need more practice. XP is a game reward, not a reading score.

    How time is counted

    Reading and teaching time is confirmed by the next answer, Ready, replay, continue, Home or pause action. After 30 seconds without a game action, the game pauses and discards the unconfirmed interval. Menus, results, background tabs and device sleep do not count. Idle excluded shows discarded reading intervals; paused and background duration is not measured. Assessment and demo time are shown separately and do not advance Pip’s growth.

    This measures interaction-confirmed time, not attention. A reading interval longer than 30 seconds is conservatively excluded. Closing or reloading can lose the last unconfirmed interval.

    Earlier time records could include waiting. They are kept separately and do not count towards growth: .

    Word selection

    Chapter 1 uses 30 story words from the mixed BlitzWord Core 200 pool. Core 200 is a curriculum selection, not the 200 most frequent English words: it combines high-frequency words, phonics examples and forest vocabulary. In the supplied workbook, water has Fry rank 84; bird is selected for phonics and the story theme. Assessment and saved learning records are unchanged.

    Multiplication time

    The bonus uses 60 unpaused, foreground seconds. Pause, Home, hidden tabs and device sleep stop the countdown; returning does not refill it. New rounds score +1 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong answer, including below zero. Earned XP is permanent; correct answers still earn 1 XP. Older personal records and any round already in progress retain their original scoring. Keypad actions confirm active multiplication time separately from reading, and both count towards growth. Unconfirmed time at the deadline is excluded from active play.
